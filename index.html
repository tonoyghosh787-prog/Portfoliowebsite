Hi, This Is Tonoy.

I forge clean topology and make PBR shaders talk.

If you're exploring this system, you'll see a 3D Prop Artist. But under the hood? I'm obsessed with the intersection of real-time game optimization and tactile visual storytelling.

From modeling complex hard-surface weapons and modular Mars habitats to designing commercial 3D packaging and trade-show environments, I love the technical puzzle of non-overlapping UV layouts, cage baking, and draw-call optimization just as much as sculpting the final silhouette.
